Unit 1: Polynomial and Rational Functions

Topic: Average Rates of Change (AROC)

Question 1: Given the function $f(x) = 3x^2 - 2x + 1$, what is the average rate of change of $f$ over the interval $[1, 4]$?

View Answer & Explanation

Correct Answer: B) 13

Explanation: Calculate $f(1) = 3(1)^2 - 2(1) + 1 = 2$ and $f(4) = 3(4)^2 - 2(4) + 1 = 41$. Use the AROC formula $\frac{f(4) - f(1)}{4 - 1} = \frac{41 - 2}{3} = \frac{39}{3} = 13$.
